## v1 (draft)

112 characters.

## Critique against the judge brief

v1 fails criterion 5 (fresh): "touch grass" and "main-character syndrome"
are the usual chatbot jokes the judge has read a thousand times. The
clinical Diagnosis/Prescription frame is right — calm therapist voice,
deadpan finding — but the finding itself is predictable, so the turn does
not land at the end. Revision: keep the clinical frame, replace the
finding with one the reader cannot see coming.

## v2 (final)

> Diagnosis: terminal people-pleasing. Prognosis: you will RSVP yes to your own funeral, then apologise for the inconvenience.

124 characters. The clinical frame stays, the finding is now one nobody
predicts — RSVPing yes to your own funeral — and the laugh lands on the
last clause, "apologise for the inconvenience", which is the most
people-pleasing thing a person could do at their own funeral.
